A show for every song, without the hundred hours.

Sequencing one song by hand can eat a whole weekend, and it helps to have an ear for music. This does the sequencing for you. Give it your S6 preview and a song, and you get back a show that knows your arches from your snowflakes.

Runs on this computer. Your preview and your music never leave it.

  1. 1

    Your preview

    The .lorprev you already have. It is the map: every prop, its channels, and where it stands in the yard.

  2. 2

    Your song

    Any MP3, WAV or M4A. It finds the beats, the bars, the loud parts, the sleigh bells and the cymbal crashes.

  3. 3

    Your show

    A native S6 .loredit. Play it as it is, or open it in S6 and make it yours.
