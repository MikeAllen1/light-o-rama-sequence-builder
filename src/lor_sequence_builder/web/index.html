A show for every song, without the hundred hours.

Sequencing one song by hand can eat a whole weekend, and it helps to have an ear for music. This does the sequencing for you. Give it your display and a song: an S5 or S6 preview, or with S4, one of your sequences. You get back a show that knows your arches from your snowflakes.

    2. 2

      Any MP3, WAV or M4A. It finds the beats, the bars, the loud parts, the sleigh bells and the cymbal crashes.

      Your show

      A native sequence: .loredit for S5 and S6, .lms for S4. Play it as it is, or open it in the Sequencer and make it yours.

    New prop this year?

    Add it to the shows you already have instead of starting over. Everything already in the sequence stays exactly as it is. Only the new props get effects, in the sequence's own style, on its beat, and dark wherever it goes dark.

    S5/S6: choose the finished .loredit and your updated preview with the new props. S4: add the new channels to the .lms in the S4 Sequencer, leave them empty, and choose that file. The song is optional; with it, the new props follow the music more closely.
