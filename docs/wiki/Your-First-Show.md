# Your first show

You need your display's **S5/S6 preview** (`.lorprev`) — or, with S4, one of your `.lms` sequences — and a **song** (MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG, WMA or AIFF).

1. Open **LOR Sequencer** and click **New show**.
2. **1 · Your display:** choose the preview. The panel says what it found ("7 arches, 14 snowflakes, 1 mega tree") and lists any prop it cannot sequence, with the reason.
3. **2 · The song:** choose the audio file. It is analyzed for beats, bars, parts of the song, strong hits, sleigh bells and cymbal crashes, in a few seconds to half a minute.
4. **3 · Requirements (optional):** skip this the first time. See [Requirements](Requirements).
5. **4 · Generate:** pick a [style](Styles) and click **Generate show**, or **Make all three styles** to compare them side by side.
6. Read the **score card**: the quality score, whether every rule passed, how much of the yard is lit, and anything worth a look. **Show report** has the details.
7. Click **Open in the workspace** (or **Yard view**) to watch the show play on a drawing of your yard with the song.
8. **Save show**, put the song in S6's audio folder under the same name, and open the `.loredit` in the Sequencer.

Next time, your display and style are already chosen: just pick a song.

## What the show does

- Arches chase on the beat, mini trees trade beats, snowflakes fall (on the sleigh bells when there are any), the mega tree spins, faces sing, outlines and stars glow underneath.
- Quiet parts use a few props; loud parts use more; the whole yard is saved for the biggest moments.
- The song's strongest hits light a chunk of the yard; crashes flash; a returning chorus looks like it did the first time.
- Classic darkens the yard for a beat just before the last big chorus; Classic and Elegant end by twinkling out.

## A whole playlist

After choosing your display, **Choose songs** under *A whole playlist at once* builds one show per song and **Save all shows** downloads them in one zip.

## Tips for better shows

- Give props a real S6 shape (Arch, Tree, Star, Snowflake) rather than plain lines.
- Group rows in the S6 preview (all the arches in one group, in order): groups and their order decide chase order.
- Set the start location of multi-segment props so spins and wipes run the right way.
