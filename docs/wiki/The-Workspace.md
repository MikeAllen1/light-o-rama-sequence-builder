# The workspace

The workspace shows a sequence the way the Light-O-Rama Sequencer does — one row per channel, effects as blocks — with the song's waveform and the beats, bars and parts it found above.

- **Open sequence** and **Open audio** to look at any S4/S5/S6 sequence with its song; **Yard view** (or **Y**) plays it on a drawing of your yard.
- Select, move, trim, draw, delete, duplicate, undo and redo effects, with snapping to the music. **Export edited copy** (or **Save sequence** for a show the app just made) writes a new file after a safety check; your original is never changed.
- A **music review** lets you correct beats, bars, crashes, hits, section names and lyrics, and save them for the next show.
- Lyrics can be loaded from LRC, SRT or VTT files.

Keyboard: **Space** or **K** play/pause, **J**/**L** back/forward 5 seconds, **Home** start, **Y** yard view, **?** all shortcuts.

For real fine-tuning, use the S6 Sequencer: it is the full editor.
