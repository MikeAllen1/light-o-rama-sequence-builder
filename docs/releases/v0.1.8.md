# LOR Sequencer 0.1.8

## How to install

1. **[Download LOR-Sequencer-Setup.exe](https://github.com/MikeAllen1/light-o-rama-sequence-builder/releases/latest/download/LOR-Sequencer-Setup.exe)** (or click LOR-Sequencer-Setup.exe under Assets at the bottom of this page).
2. Double-click it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Click through the installer. It puts LOR Sequencer on your Start menu. Already have it? Run the new one over it; your settings are kept.

**Don't download "Source code (zip)" or "Source code (tar.gz)".** GitHub adds those to every release; they are for programmers and will not run.

Can't run installers on your PC? Download [LOR-Sequencer-Portable.zip](https://github.com/MikeAllen1/light-o-rama-sequence-builder/releases/latest/download/LOR-Sequencer-Portable.zip), right-click it, choose **Extract All**, open the folder and double-click **LOR Sequencer.exe**.

## What's new

- Lights that play the song: its strongest hits, on the beat or between, light a share of the yard, so beat-heavy songs like Wizards in Winter land their accents
- A fuller, livelier yard: arches and the mega tree rest on a twinkle or shimmer between moves, snowflakes keep falling in the background, and outlines no longer sit still
- Mega tree star: a new "Keep channels lit with the rest" requirement keeps the star (and the channel shared with your roofline) lit with the tree and never alone
- The three styles are clearly different: Elegant stays calm through loud choruses, Classic pulses on beats one and three, High energy drives every beat and saves its brightest for the loud parts
- Choruses look different from verses; Classic ripples sometimes mirror out from the middle
- Words that light props: a new "Light when these words are sung" requirement (for example "fire" lights the homemade trees); a WhisperX word file now cues props as well as Santa's mouth
- Each show's notes list what it is made of: native LOR effects, moves built from them, and nothing your controllers can't show

Everything runs on your computer; nothing is sent anywhere.
