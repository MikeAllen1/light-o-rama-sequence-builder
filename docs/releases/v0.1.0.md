# LOR Sequencer 0.1.0

The first release: give it your Light-O-Rama S6 preview and a song, and it builds a native S6 sequence for your display.

- Build a show in a few clicks: pick a style (Classic, High energy or Elegant), add requirements like "keep the star on", and generate.
- Every kind of prop gets a job: arches chase on the beat, mini trees trade beats, snowflakes fall, the mega tree spins, faces sing (with the vocals add-on).
- Watch it first on a drawing of your yard, then save the .loredit and play it in S6.
- A whole playlist at once: one show per song, saved together.
- A 10-second display test to check your wiring before the real show.
- Copy diagnostics on the start page when something goes wrong.
- Runs on your computer; your preview and music never leave it.

Download LOR-Sequencer-Setup-0.1.0.exe below and run it. No administrator rights are needed. Cannot run installers? LOR-Sequencer-Portable-0.1.0.zip: unzip it and run LOR Sequencer.exe.
