# LOR Sequencer 0.1.10

## How to install

1. **[Download LOR-Sequencer-Setup.exe](https://github.com/MikeAllen1/light-o-rama-sequence-builder/releases/latest/download/LOR-Sequencer-Setup.exe)** (or click LOR-Sequencer-Setup.exe under Assets at the bottom of this page).
2. Double-click it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Click through the installer. It puts LOR Sequencer on your Start menu. Already have it? Run the new one over it; your settings are kept.

**Don't download "Source code (zip)" or "Source code (tar.gz)".** GitHub adds those to every release; they are for programmers and will not run.

Can't run installers on your PC? Download [LOR-Sequencer-Portable.zip](https://github.com/MikeAllen1/light-o-rama-sequence-builder/releases/latest/download/LOR-Sequencer-Portable.zip), right-click it, choose **Extract All**, open the folder and double-click **LOR Sequencer.exe**.

## What's new

- Classic, closer still to hand-made shows:
  - The star beats along instead of staying on
  - The mega tree spins over a softly lit tree instead of a dark one
  - Decorations flicker across the yard on the beat instead of all jumping at once

Everything runs on your computer; nothing is sent anywhere.
