# LOR Sequencer 0.1.7

## How to install

1. **[Download LOR-Sequencer-Setup.exe](https://github.com/MikeAllen1/light-o-rama-sequence-builder/releases/latest/download/LOR-Sequencer-Setup.exe)** (or click LOR-Sequencer-Setup.exe under Assets at the bottom of this page).
2. Double-click it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Click through the installer. It puts LOR Sequencer on your Start menu. Already have it? Run the new one over it; your settings are kept.

**Don't download "Source code (zip)" or "Source code (tar.gz)".** GitHub adds those to every release; they are for programmers and will not run.

Can't run installers on your PC? Download [LOR-Sequencer-Portable.zip](https://github.com/MikeAllen1/light-o-rama-sequence-builder/releases/latest/download/LOR-Sequencer-Portable.zip), right-click it, choose **Extract All**, open the folder and double-click **LOR Sequencer.exe**.

## What's new

- Fuller shows that move in groups: every effect lands on the song's sixteenth notes, so props hit together; props that aren't leading keep a glow and pulse on the beat; fills and chases step on the music (calibrated against professional sequences)
- Wiring rules for any display: props wired to the same circuit always show the same light; a new "Light whole" requirement keeps every channel of a prop together (two-color mini arches never light one side alone); a singing face always shows exactly one mouth with its outline
- Lip-sync from a WhisperX word file: pick who the face sings, and the mouth opens on each syllable
- Song parts by what repeats: verses, choruses and the bridge are found from the music, and the bridge gets its own look
- No more black console windows when choosing a song; recent shows say which style and version made them
- A direct download link and plain install steps

Everything runs on your computer; nothing is sent anywhere.

Free to use, not to sell.
