# LOR Sequencer 0.1.11

## How to install

1. **[Download LOR-Sequencer-Setup.exe](https://github.com/MikeAllen1/light-o-rama-sequence-builder/releases/latest/download/LOR-Sequencer-Setup.exe)** (or click LOR-Sequencer-Setup.exe under Assets at the bottom of this page).
2. Double-click it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Click through the installer. It puts LOR Sequencer on your Start menu. Already have it? Run the new one over it; your settings are kept.

**Don't download "Source code (zip)" or "Source code (tar.gz)".** GitHub adds those to every release; they are for programmers and will not run.

Can't run installers on your PC? Download [LOR-Sequencer-Portable.zip](https://github.com/MikeAllen1/light-o-rama-sequence-builder/releases/latest/download/LOR-Sequencer-Portable.zip), right-click it, choose **Extract All**, open the folder and double-click **LOR Sequencer.exe**.

**Mac** (Apple silicon, macOS 14 or later): download [LOR-Sequencer-macOS.zip](https://github.com/MikeAllen1/light-o-rama-sequence-builder/releases/latest/download/LOR-Sequencer-macOS.zip), double-click it and drag **LOR Sequencer** into Applications. The first time, macOS says it cannot check the app: click **Done**, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

**Linux** (64-bit): download [LOR-Sequencer-x86_64.AppImage](https://github.com/MikeAllen1/light-o-rama-sequence-builder/releases/latest/download/LOR-Sequencer-x86_64.AppImage), allow it to run as a program, and double-click it. It opens in your browser.

## What's new

- **Mac and Linux apps.** LOR Sequencer now comes for Mac (in its own window) and Linux (in your browser), with everything it needs inside, FFmpeg too. Make shows on any computer and copy them to the one that runs Light-O-Rama.
- **Sequences open whatever version saved them.** The program no longer checks the save-file version: S5/S6 and S4 sequences are told apart by what is in them, and a sequence you save back keeps its version.
- The "new version" note links to the download for your kind of computer.

Everything runs on your computer; nothing is sent anywhere.

Free to use, not to sell.
